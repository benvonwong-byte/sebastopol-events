How this works

Every morning a script reads the public calendars of the groups listed here, the ones people add through this site, the Sonoma County Library, and the Luma, Eventbrite and Meetup listings for this part of the county. It keeps what happens in Sebastopol (ZIP 95472) and in the towns within about 22 km, and merges anything listed twice. Check the organizer's page before you go.

    Add to the calendar

    Get your events here

    Paste the link to wherever your group keeps its events. We read it every morning, so new events appear here on their own.

    Works with Luma, Google Calendar (a public calendar), Meetup, Eventbrite, Mobilize, Tockify, any .ics or webcal link, RSS feeds, and Squarespace or WordPress events pages.

    Email it instead

    "Send it" opens a short form on GitHub, where a free account is needed. A bot checks the link within a minute and replies with what it found.

    For a one-off. For anything that repeats, saves you posting each one.

    Email it instead

    If your group posts events only on Instagram, a flyer or a web page of text, five minutes with one of these puts every future event here.

    Luma (easiest)

    1. Go to luma.com and sign up free.
    2. Create a calendar for your group, then add events to it.
    3. Paste the calendar's page link under .

    Google Calendar

    1. In Google Calendar, make a new calendar for your group's public events.
    2. In its settings, under "Access permissions", tick "Make available to public".
    3. Copy "Public address in iCal format" and paste it under .

    Already have a website?

    Squarespace and WordPress (with The Events Calendar) events pages work as they are: paste the events page link under . So do Meetup, Eventbrite organizer pages, and any page with an RSS feed of events.
